## Titles and thumbnail copy work together

- Base the title on the video's actual question, demonstration or payoff. Put the recognizable subject and strongest useful hook early so mobile truncation does not hide the point. Use natural Bengali for a Bengali audience; an English search phrase can help when relevant, not as a mandatory suffix.
- Create curiosity while accurately representing the content. Do not promise a universal percentage drop, instant fix, guaranteed result or test the video did not perform. A number from an illustrative scenario must not become a claim that every device behaves that way.
- Let the thumbnail deliver a brief visual hook and the title explain the subject or deepen the question; they need not repeat every word. Avoid keyword stuffing, excessive punctuation and a long generic channel suffix. Check the destination's actual title limit before publishing rather than imposing one universal character count across platforms/languages.
- When title alternatives are requested, give the requested count with meaningfully different angles, such as a question, a concrete comparison or a mechanism reveal. Do not make a claim of higher click-through performance without measured evidence.
