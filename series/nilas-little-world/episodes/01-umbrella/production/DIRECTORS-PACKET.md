# Nila's Little World — Episode 01: An Umbrella Story

## Outline
Nila dreams of sharing her art. On the way to an exhibition she notices a flower seller struggling in the rain. She chooses to stop, shelters the flowers with her umbrella, and makes a small illustrated sign. Customers begin to stop. At home, the seller shares food and a new exercise book with his daughter. Nila continues to the exhibition, unaware that her work has already reached someone.
